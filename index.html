Who we are

Fruit, chosen the way we'd choose it for our own table.

How ordering works

Three steps, no guesswork

Browse and select

Pick your fruits and add them to your order. Quantities can be adjusted at any time before you send.

Review and send on WhatsApp

Add your name, contact and delivery location. We prepare a ready-to-send message — you press Send inside WhatsApp.

We confirm, then deliver

Our team confirms availability, any range prices, delivery fees and timing with you directly before the order is completed.

Nationwide delivery

We deliver all over Uganda.

Wherever you are — Kampala, a neighbouring district or further afield — we can discuss delivery with you directly.

Delivery information

  • Delivery is arranged and confirmed with you on WhatsApp before your order is finalised.
  • Delivery fees depend on your location and order — they are not calculated on this website.
  • Estimated delivery times are confirmed by our team when we speak with you.
  • Questions about your area? Message us and we will let you know.
