About our prices

Some fruits are priced on confirmation.

Local Lemon, Small Bananas, Peaches, Plums and Pawpaw are listed as a price range because the final amount depends on size and quantity. These are never given a made-up subtotal on this website.

  • Range-priced items show a clear "final price confirmed on WhatsApp" note.
  • Your order summary only totals fixed-price items.
  • Our team confirms the final amount for range-priced items before the order is completed.
