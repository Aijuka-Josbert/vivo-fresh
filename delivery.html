Delivery & Payment

We deliver all over Uganda.

Delivery is arranged with each customer individually. Because fees and timing depend on your location and your order, both are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before your order is finalised.

Nationwide Delivery

We arrange delivery to Kampala and all neighbouring districts. Just send us your location on WhatsApp.

Cash on Delivery

No online payment needed. You inspect your fresh produce and pay in cash when it arrives at your door.

The Process

From our market stall to your door.

We keep the process simple so you spend less time on your phone and more time enjoying your food.

1

Build your order

Browse the catalogue and add the items you want. Adjust quantities at any point before you send.

2

Send it on WhatsApp

Add your name and delivery location. The site prepares a message — you press Send inside WhatsApp to confirm.

3

We confirm & deliver

Our team replies to confirm availability, delivery fees and timing. Then we bring it to your door.

What we confirm with you

  • Delivery fee: Depends on your location and order size. Not calculated on this website.
  • Delivery time: Confirmed by our team when we speak with you.
  • Product availability: Fresh produce changes daily, so we check before confirming.
  • Final price: Items with variable sizes (like Cabbage or Peaches) will have their price confirmed.

A note on honesty

No hidden fees. No fake timelines.

We deliberately do not display fixed delivery charges or delivery windows on this website. Anything we showed here would be a guess — and a guess is not something you should plan your day around. We confirm the real numbers with you directly via WhatsApp.

  • Our Promises:
  • We confirm all fees before you pay.
  • You only pay cash when the goods arrive.
  • If an item is out of stock, we tell you immediately.
  • We do not add hidden charges to your subtotal.

Questions about your area?

Message our team on WhatsApp with your location and we will let you know how delivery works for you.
