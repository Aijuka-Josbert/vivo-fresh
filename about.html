About VIVO FRESH

Fresh produce, made simple to buy.

We are a fresh produce business based at Nakasero Market in Kampala, Uganda. Our work is straightforward: bring together a genuinely varied selection of quality fruits, vegetables, spices and meat, and make it easy for customers across Uganda to order and receive it.

Who we are

A market stall with a wider reach.

VIVO FRESH @ UGANDA operates from Nakasero Market, one of Kampala's busiest and best-known fresh produce markets. Trading from the market keeps us close to what is actually in season and available — and it means customers get real, current produce rather than a static list that never changes.

Our catalogue currently covers 133 products: local staples like avocados, mangoes, and Sukuma Wiki alongside imported and exotic finds such as blueberries, dragon fruit, and fresh meat. We are constantly updating our range to match what is best at the market.

Alongside the produce itself, we offer a simpler way to buy it. Customers can browse and build an order on this website, then send it to our team on WhatsApp. Everything else — availability, any range prices, delivery fees and timing — is confirmed directly with the customer before the order is finalised.

What we offer

Produce, and the convenience around it.

A varied catalogue of 133 products, clear unit pricing, an order-building tool that works on a phone, and a direct line to our team on WhatsApp. Delivery is available across Uganda, arranged with each customer individually.

Our approach to quality

Availability is confirmed, not assumed.

Fresh produce changes daily. Rather than promising stock we cannot guarantee, we confirm availability with you when you send your order. If something is not available, we will tell you — and discuss alternatives.

Our direction

Vision, mission and goals

The statements below are drafted wording for this website and are pending review by the business owner. They are not presented as official company declarations.

Our Vision

To make quality, fresh produce accessible and convenient for customers throughout Uganda — so that ordering good food is never limited by distance or circumstance.

Our Mission

To provide a convenient shopping experience, with a diverse selection of quality produce, honest pricing and clear communication from the moment an order is placed to the moment it arrives.

Our Goals

  • Provide a wide variety of fresh fruits, vegetables, and meats, covering both everyday favourites and less common varieties.
  • Improve customer convenience through a simple, mobile-friendly way to order.
  • Expand accessibility by arranging delivery across Uganda.
  • Maintain customer satisfaction through clear communication and careful handling of each order.
  • Grow a trusted retail brand that customers return to.
