Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We do not have a database, and we do not store your personal information on this website. When you build an order, your name, phone number, location, and order details are temporarily saved in your own browser (using localStorage) just so you don't lose your cart if you refresh the page.

2. WhatsApp Communication

When you click "Send order via WhatsApp", your browser opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message. You must press "Send" inside the WhatsApp app. Once you do, we receive your details via WhatsApp. Their privacy policy applies to that message.

3. How We Use Your Information

We only use the information you send us to process your order, arrange delivery, and communicate with you about availability and pricing. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

4. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics, or third-party advertising scripts. It only uses local browser storage to remember your shopping cart.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at amongivivian123@gmail.com.
