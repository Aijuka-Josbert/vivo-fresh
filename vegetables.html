About our prices

Some items are priced on confirmation.

Cabbage, Lettuce, Tomatoes, Matoke, Spring Onions, Leeks, Garlic Imported and a few others are listed as a price range because the final amount depends on size and quantity. These never get a made-up subtotal on this website.

  • Range-priced items show a clear "final price confirmed on WhatsApp" note.
  • Your order summary only totals fixed-price items.
  • Our team confirms the final amount for range-priced items before the order is completed.
